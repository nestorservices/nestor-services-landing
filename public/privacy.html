Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Nestor Services uses your personal data only to help you find employment and to help employers hire you, and never for anything else. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it and the rights you have.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Nestor Services is a talent acquisition firm based in Gurugram, India. It is a sole proprietorship of Shashank Malviya, registered in Uttar Pradesh. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the DPDP Act), we are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here.

This policy applies to:

  • Candidates who apply for jobs we advertise, are approached by us, or take an assessment we send.
  • Visitors to nestorservices.in and hire.nestorservices.in.
  • Client contacts (people at the companies we hire for) whose business details we hold.

Business contact: Nestor Services, Gurugram, Haryana, India. Privacy and grievance contact: hire@nestorservices.in.

3. Why we use your data, and on what basis

We use your data only for employment-related purposes, and only with your consent.

We use it to:

  1. Review your application against the role you applied for.
  2. Send you assessments and interview invitations, and evaluate your results.
  3. Share your profile with the hiring company for that role (section 4).
  4. Keep you informed about the status of your application.
  5. Tell you about other suitable roles, only if you agree to stay in our talent pool.
  6. Keep our assessments fair and secure.
  7. Meet our legal obligations, such as responding to a lawful request from an authority.

Consent. When you apply, take an assessment or join our talent pool, we ask for your consent to these purposes. You can withdraw it at any time (section 7). Withdrawing consent stops future use, but does not undo what was lawfully done before.

We will never use your data for marketing unrelated to jobs, sell it, rent it, or use it for any purpose you have not been told about.

4. Who we share your data with

Your data goes only to the company hiring for the role you applied for, and to the service providers that help us run our process.

  • The hiring company (our client). We share your profile, assessment results and interview feedback only with the company hiring for that specific role. If a role is advertised with the company's name kept confidential, we share your details with that company once you move forward in the process. We do not send your profile to any other company without asking you first.
  • Service providers. Companies that host our websites, email and assessment platform, under contracts that allow them to process your data only on our instructions and keep it secure.
  • Authorities. Only when the law requires it, such as a valid order from a court or government authority.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data to anyone.

Some of our service providers may store data on servers outside India. Where this happens, we transfer data only as permitted under the DPDP Act.

5. Online assessments and integrity monitoring

Our assessments record a small set of events to keep them fair, and every result is reviewed by a person before any decision.

During an assessment we record:

  • When you start, move between questions and submit.
  • Your answers and code, saved automatically as you work.
  • If you leave the assessment window, or try to copy or paste (copy and paste are switched off).

We do not use your camera or microphone, take screenshots of your screen, or read anything outside the assessment window.

An integrity event (for example, leaving the window once) does not by itself lead to rejection. A Nestor Services recruiter reviews your answers and any events together, and you may be asked about them. No hiring decision is made by software alone.

7. Your rights

You can see, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent, at any time by writing to hire@nestorservices.in. We reply within 30 days.

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  1. Know what personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  2. Correct or update data that is wrong or out of date.
  3. Delete your data when it is no longer needed for the purpose you gave it for.
  4. Withdraw consent, as easily as you gave it. We will stop using your data and remove you from our talent pool.
  5. Raise a grievance with us (section 8), and if you are not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India.
  6. Nominate a person to exercise these rights for you in case of death or incapacity.

To protect you, we may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request. We never charge a fee for any of these requests.
